About

Quantitative by training,
curious by nature.

I completed my M.Sc. in Statistics in 2025 and passed the IAI's ACET with distinction — the entry exam into India's actuarial profession. Since then I've been building a practical analytics skill set on top of that theory: SQL, Python, Power BI and cloud data warehousing, alongside three actuarial papers I'm sitting in November 2026.
